## Call for Workshops

Ph0wn 2027 is looking for **enthusiast speakers** to present **free hands-on-labs** on hacking/ security/ embedded systems. 

- <i class="fa-solid fa-calendar"></i> Date: **Friday 12 March, 2027** afternoon.
- <i class="fa-solid fa-location-dot"></i> Venue: Onsite, [EURECOM, Sophia Antipolis](https://ph0wn.org/venue), France
- <i class="fa-solid fa-clock"></i> Duration: From 2 hours to 3 hour 50. *In 2027, we may be able to accomodate a 6 hour workshop* (talk to us).
- <i class="fa-solid fa-language"></i> Language: French or English (your choice). Please only select English if your English is *good*.
- <i class="fa-solid fa-people-group"></i> Audience size: Let us know how many participants you can reasonably handle. We expect something between 10 and 60. The most usual headcount is 20-25.

Ph0wn workshops are mostly **hands-on labs**. Very few slides, but **lots of exercises**.

### Ideas of topics

Ph0wn covers security and hacking topics for embedded systems, mobile phones, connected objects, industrial objects, artificial intelligence and miscellaneous platforms (satellite, retro-computing).

**Any workshop on legal tools, languages, methodology to develop, hack, test, repair, investigate those systems** is perfect for us (as long as it remains [legal](/conduct) !).

Examples :

- Using pwntools to exploit CTF challenges
- Setting up a hardware key to secure your Linux session and GPG keys
- How to use a logic analyzer
- Developing your first quick app on a VR headset

We also welcome workshops on more miscellaneous topics like **astronomy, 8-bit music, repairing mechanical pinballs, astromech robots** etc. 


### Compensations for the Speaker(s)

Workshops take a *lot of time to prepare, setup and test*. We truly appreciate it and thank all our speakers warmly. However, due to limited sponsorship, we are **usually unable to cover any speaker expenses**. If your case is special (e.g unemployed, limited resources), let us know, and we'll do all we can to fund you.

If you are selected, we will

- Advertize your workshop
- If relevant, promote your company, website, tool
- Find a nice Goodie for you (very often, it's an electronic device)
- VIP Status: we invite you to Ph0wn's social event and CTF

<i class="fa-solid fa-sack-dollar"></i> Important: Ph0wn workshops are **free** for all attendees. You are **not authorized to sell anything during your workshop** (university rules).

>- <i class="fa-solid fa-address-card"></i> First Name, Last Name, Email address

## Call for Demos

Ph0wn 2027 is looking for **5-minute visual live demos** on hacking/ security/ embedded systems topics, to show to the Ph0wn audience. This is how it works:

1.  <i class="fa fa-laptop"></i> The speaker sets up all necessary equipment. The room is available 1 hour an advance. We provide a video projector and Internet access, if you need anything else, please ask us if we have that.

2. The speaker shows the demo. It's a live demo : s/he may interact with devices, or even ask the audience to do so. <i class="fa fa-hourglass"></i> The demo lasts **5 minutes maximum**. **Like for Rump Sessions, the demo is cut in the middle if goes beyond its time slot**.

3. <i class="fa fa-comment"></i> If the demo is shorter than 5 minutes, the remaining time may be used to answer Questions from the Audience.

>- <i class="fa-solid fa-calendar"></i> Date: **Friday 12 March, 2027** afternoon.
>- <i class="fa-solid fa-location-dot"></i> Venue: Onsite, [EURECOM, Sophia Antipolis](https://ph0wn.org/venue), France
>- <i class="fa-solid fa-language"></i> Language: English is preferred, but French is possible too.

### Conditions

- Commercial pitches are forbidden.
- **We don't accept demos of products/tools you are selling commercially**. The only exception is for Open Source tools.
- We prefer **live demos** to videos. If your live demo fails repeatedly (it happens...), a backup video is nevertheless a good idea.
- Your presentation is **free** to attend. You will not receive any compensation, except glory, our warm thanks and a guaranteed seat to the CTF.
- It's a **demo** not a slide deck! We will cut any presentation with more than 2 slides.

### Topics

Acceptable topics are [the same as for workshops](#ideas-of-topics). We also welcome failure cases and fun demos (they must remain respectful and legal - see [Ph0wn's Code of Conduct](/conduct)).

Examples:

- How you solved CTF challenge xyz
- How you designed a 3D printed trophy

Please send us an [email](mailto:contact @ ph0wn . org) and explain shortly what you want to demo.
